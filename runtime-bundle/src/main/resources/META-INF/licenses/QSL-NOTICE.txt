The selected official 10.0.0-alpha.5+1.21.1 module JARs declare Apache-2.0 in
quilt.mod.json but do not contain a separate license-text file. The host supplies
the standard Apache-2.0 text here without modifying those original payloads.
Their original IDs, versions, contributor/contact metadata and resources remain.
Selection and hashes are recorded in META-INF/unified-infinity/bundled-qsl.json.
Bundling alone does not certify runtime compatibility of every QSL module.
