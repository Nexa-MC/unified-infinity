The driver keeps a 42-minute work deadline and a 2,520-second shared graphics lifetime, sufficient for both serial 650-second pair budgets. TERM, INT and HUP are deferred across collector creation, pidfd acquisition and the driver handle handoff; the collector runner checks for pending stops only where its cleanup owns the child. Repeated stop signals are deferred through cleanup and final reporting. All three signals trigger ordered cleanup: stop and wait up to 30 seconds for the retained owned pair supervisor before stopping the graphics collector (up to 20 seconds). If the pair cannot be reaped, graphics teardown is skipped and acceptance fails closed. Cleanup failures are recorded without replacing the original failure or suppressing the final receipt. This leaves the remaining three minutes of the 45-minute job for cleanup. The driver prints text receipts and bounded failure/game log tails. There is no artifact upload, binary log encoding, Actions cache, secret use, paid service or larger runner. The single standard Ubuntu24 job has a 45-minute hard timeout.
